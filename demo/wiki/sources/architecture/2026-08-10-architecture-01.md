## Summary

llmwiki implements a three-layer architecture adapted from [[Karpathy]]'s LLM Wiki gist: `raw/` (immutable source), `wiki/` (LLM-synthesized content), and `site/` (generated static HTML). The wiki layer contains synthesized session summaries, entities, concepts, and projects that compound over time. Candidates for new entities and concepts require human-or-agent review before promotion to trusted hubs; synthesis alone can complete the raw layer while leaving the knowledge layer empty.

## Key Claims

- llmwiki adopts a three-layer architecture (raw → wiki → site) directly from [[Karpathy]]'s LLM Wiki model
- The raw/ layer is immutable and entirely converter-maintained; source errors must be fixed in the converter, never in the output
- The wiki/ layer is entirely LLM-maintained and contains synthesized summaries (sources/), entities, concepts, projects, and candidate stubs (candidates/)
- The site/ layer is fully generated by `llmwiki build` and safe to delete and regenerate at any time
- Candidates require human-or-agent review before promotion to entity/concept hubs; raw synthesis alone cannot complete the knowledge layer without this gate

## Key Quotes

> "Everything under `raw/` is treated as source-of-truth. The converter writes to it; nothing else should. If a source is wrong, fix the converter, not the output."
— Establishes immutability and the single point of correction

> "Synthesis alone can leave Home looking 'finished' (Raw → Synthesized) while the knowledge layer is still empty"
— Distinguishes between completing raw synthesis and completing the knowledge base; both are required for a full wiki

> "Pages compound over time — every new source makes the wiki richer."
— Describes the additive, non-destructive knowledge accumulation model
